Topics of Interest

We welcome theoretical and empirical papers on topics including, but not limited to:

  • The economic drivers of climate-induced migration and displacement
  • Impacts of climate migration on labor markets, wages, and inequality in both sending and receiving regions
  • The role of policy in managing climate migration
  • Methodological advances in modeling and measuring the climate-migration relation
  • The fiscal and social impacts of climate-related population movements
  • The financial implications of climate-induced migration and displacement
